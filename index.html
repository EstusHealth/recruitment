Career Architecture

Four pathways. You choose.

Not everyone wants the same thing. Four distinct tracks so you can lead in the way that fits. And yes, you can explore more than one.

Clinical Lead

Depth Track

Deep clinical expertise. Lead complex caseloads, supervise junior clinicians, shape protocols, and become the specialist others refer to.

Team Lead

People Track

Lead a clinical pod. Manage team performance, coordinate caseloads, mentor new staff, and own the rhythm of your team.

Practice Manager

Operations Track

Revenue modelling, service design, hiring, systems. Build the infrastructure that lets great clinicians do great work.

Thought Leader

Content Track

Write, speak, build an audience. Publish clinical insights, present at conferences, and build your professional brand.

Our Philosophy

We actively invest in the next generation of leaders.

Here's something most employers won't say: we want you to outgrow us. The accelerator is designed so that after 2-3 years, you have every option available. Stay and lead at Estus. Move into another organisation as a senior clinician. Or start your own practice with the business literacy, clinical depth, and confidence to actually make it work.

We don't see that as losing staff. We see it as our job. Allied health is stronger when it's led by clinicians who've been properly developed, not just credentialed. Estus Health exists to build those clinicians. If you go on to create something extraordinary, that's not a loss. That's the whole point.

Questions

You're probably wondering

Can I pursue more than one pathway?
Yes. The four pathways aren't locked tracks. Most people will develop skills across two or three of them. The structure exists so you can see where your strengths are developing and lean into the one that fits you best. Plenty of great clinical leads also write and speak. Plenty of team leads also think commercially. The pathways are a framework, not a cage.
What if I want to start my own practice afterwards?
That's not a risk. That's one of the best outcomes. The accelerator is specifically designed to give you the clinical, operational, and financial literacy to build your own thing if that's what you want. We'll actively support you through that transition. A generation of well-trained, confident practice owners lifting the standard of allied health? That's exactly what we're trying to build.
Do I need to be a new grad to apply?
The program is designed primarily for final-year students, new graduates, and early-career OTs. If you're a few years in but feel like you missed the structured development you needed, you're welcome to reach out. We'll have an honest conversation about whether the accelerator is the right fit.
